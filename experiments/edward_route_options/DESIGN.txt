Five paired arms, identical checkpoint160,11 saved states,original RNG and remaining512-step budget:
A native: unchanged original Edward.
B native_step1: only commit_moves=1, unchanged native candidates/ranking.
C multi_step1: previous basic multi-route-assisted single-step prototype (not complete route execution).
D escape_step1: C plus bounded post-pellet escape viability.
E bound_segments: D family with explicit route registry, geometric summaries, bounded route execution and route audit. E changes a package: route retention, richer prompt, route candidate diversity, auxiliary route materialization and segment boundaries; cannot attribute E-D solely to multi-route generation.
A vs B isolates per-step model decision. C vs D isolates escape guard. B vs C changes route generation and short safety together (not individually isolated). All model and CPU results reported separately.
E route identity hashes origin,target,full actions,portal edges,strategy. Model receives route_id plus all junction choices,turns,portal entry/exit,approach cell/side and next segment boundary. It does not receive RNG or a guarantee of entire-route safety.
E continuation follows exact chosen action sequence; no candidate regeneration during the segment. Stop after at most4 actions or at junction/portal exit/target. Post-step checks evaluate the next action before execution (simulator is paused between actions). A mismatch or predicted risk invalidates the option; model is recalled. Each executed action and origin must match its bound route. Native risk fallback is explicitly risky and remains one step.
